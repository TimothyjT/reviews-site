What does Review Autopilot do?

  1. You give the go-ahead and a link to your public review profile.
  2. We pull your reviews and sort every unhappy reviewer (1–3 stars) HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW for recovery, by rules printed in the audit — how recent the review is, whether you’ve responded, whether the complaint is specific, whether the reviewer says it was resolved.
  3. We draft the private recovery messages and the public replies, hand-check each one against the review it answers, and set up the request flow and your testimonial widget.
  4. You receive the audit as a document. You read, edit, and send. Nothing goes out in your name without you.

A sample moment — shown with made-up names

Sample: a two-star review lands — “waited three weeks, nobody called back.” It sorts HIGH: recent, specific, unanswered. The draft in your audit reads: “James, thank you for telling us — we’re sorry nobody called you back. Waiting three weeks for a call that never came isn’t right. Please contact us directly and ask for the owner; we’ll go over what happened and put it right.” You change two words, post it, and send the private note from your own records. The reviewer is never asked to change the review.

One honest note about the name: the “autopilot” is the loop, not a robot. The sorting is rules-based software, not AI; the drafts are prepared by us and checked by a person; and the sending is you. If a service in this category tells you it all happens automatically, ask it who reads the angry ones.

Who is Review Autopilot for — and who is it not for?

It’s for you if…

  • Your reviews sit unanswered for weeks because the work comes first.
  • Happy customers never get asked, so your profile is mostly the unhappy ones.
  • You want the replies written but the final say kept.

It’s not for you if…

  • You want bad reviews removed or buried. That is not offered here, at any price.
  • You want a dashboard and live alerts. This is a document and a monthly service, not software you log into.

Reviews are one half of being found. The other half is what happens when someone reaches out: missed calls are Never-Miss-a-Lead’s job, and a website that can’t answer a question is the FAQ Agent’s.

Questions

Will this get my bad reviews removed?

No — and that is not what this is. Anyone who promises removal is selling something you should not buy. Review Autopilot helps you answer well, make a genuine recovery attempt with the customers who can be reached, and earn more reviews honestly. Removal is never offered, asked for, or traded for, and nothing is ever offered in exchange for removing or changing a review.

Do you post the replies or send the messages for me?

No. Everything arrives as drafts in a document. You read them, edit them into your own voice, and you send and post them yourself. Nothing ever goes out in your name without your say-so — there is no approval app or button, because the approval is you, reading it.
